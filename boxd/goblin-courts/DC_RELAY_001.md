## Why this relay exists

This report separates four things that must not be collapsed:

1. **Record** — what an official source currently publishes.
2. **Authority** — what legal power an actor actually possesses.
3. **Execution** — what action was taken under that authority.
4. **Oversight / recovery** — what process tests, corrects, appeals, audits, or reverses the action.

A GitHub mirror, cryptographic hash, ENS name, or Base/EAS attestation may preserve a commitment to bytes. None creates legal authority or proves the truth of every claim inside those bytes.

### State judiciary

The Minnesota Supreme Court's August 5, 2026 Oslund holding is a process-friction case: a late unemployment appeal must be dismissed under the governing statutes, and the unemployment law judge lacks discretion to consider the explanation for lateness.

Goblin Court should treat this as a reusable procedural pattern:

`NOTICE -> DEADLINE -> FILING -> JURISDICTION/DISCRETION -> DISPOSITION -> APPEAL`

It demonstrates why process mechanics can control outcomes even when the underlying merits are never reached.

## Burden-of-proof membrane

The phrase "burden of proof" must always carry a class label.

- **Research/publication burden:** the person publishing a factual allegation must produce the source chain supporting that allegation.
- **Federal criminal burden:** government must prove guilt beyond a reasonable doubt; an indictment is not evidence of guilt.
- **Civil/administrative burden:** varies by claim, statute, forum, and stage.
- **Audit burden:** an official audit finding is an oversight conclusion under its audit methodology; it is not automatically a criminal adjudication.
- **BoxD burden:** no promotion beyond the receipts actually frozen and replayed.
